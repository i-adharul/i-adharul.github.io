Re-clustering 2.4M Customer Lifecycles Uncovered $3.8M in At-Risk High-LTV Accounts

01. About the project Context & Problem

E-commerceU, an enterprise retail marketplace processing 2.4M transactions annually, was experiencing deteriorating marketing ROI. Despite record user acquisition spends, customer retention had dropped 14% year-over-year. The marketing organization had been treating its customer base as an undifferentiated monolith, blasting blanket quarterly promotional discounts across all accounts.

The primary objective was to architect an automated behavioral segmentation pipeline from raw order history stored across multiple PostgreSQL replica shards. Using Python and statistical clustering methodologies, this study aims to isolate true value drivers from margin diluters, providing the growth team with distinct targeting strategies.

Dataset Scale 2,418,902 Orders
Storage Layer PostgreSQL
Pipeline Python / Polars / Scikit

02. Method (RFM) Feature Engineering

RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) transformation served as the foundation of the feature vector. However, empirical e-commerce data displays extreme positive skewness and long-tail spending profiles. Raw values directly ingested into Euclidean distance-based algorithms produce unstable cluster centroids heavily dominated by solitary wholesale accounts.

Pipeline Normalization Steps:

  • Outlier Truncation: Winsorized monetary amounts above the 99.8th percentile.
  • Log Transformation: Applied log1p transformations across Frequency and Monetary axes to enforce normal distribution symmetry.
  • StandardScaler Z-Scores: Zero-centered all axes to unity variance, equalizing distance sensitivities across days vs. currency metrics.

03. Segmentation Results Model Diagnostics & Distribution

04. Insights and Recommendations Actionable Roadmaps

INSIGHT 01

The $3.8M At-Risk Blindspot

Over 11,200 historically high-spending accounts had surpassed 120 days of inactivity without receiving tailored outreach. Recommendation: Introduce a dedicated concierge reactivation cadence with dynamic personalized inventory reminders instead of generic batch coupons.

INSIGHT 02

Ad Budget Cannibalization

Nearly 35% of performance remarketing spend targeted "Hibernating Bargain Hunters" with sub-zero margin returns due to high return rates. Recommendation: Immediately pull $420k paid social spend from this cohort and reallocate to nurture flows for "Loyal Advocates".

INSIGHT 03

Champion Repurchase Elasticity

The top 6% "Champions" generate 41% of platform GMV and exhibit almost zero price sensitivity to site-wide clearance promotions. Recommendation: Transition Champions from discount-based incentives to early access drops, VIP community tiers, and exclusive SKU bundling.

INSIGHT 04

Velocity Gaps in the Second Order Milestone

Accounts transitioning between new buyers and Loyal Advocates experienced an average latency of 68 days before purchase #2. Recommendation: Trigger automated lifecycle communications at day 21 based on category cross-sell affinities calculated from association rule mining.

INSIGHT 05

Operationalizing Cluster Drift Detection

Customer behavioral patterns shifted across holiday quarters, causing 12% cluster reassignment over 90 days. Recommendation: Schedule automated weekly Airflow DAGs to re-score RFM assignments and synchronize cohorts directly to the Customer Data Platform.
